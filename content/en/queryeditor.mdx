### Why QueryEditor

Database clients packed with panels and buttons I never touch have never really won me over. Most of them come with a bloated interface that gets in the way more than it helps, and the ones that are actually decent tend to cap your tabs on the free tier, which doesn't help either.

So at some point I decided to build my own. QueryEditor is basically what I always wanted: open the app, connect your database, and start writing SQL. No welcome screens, no setup wizards, no noise. Multiple tabs and multiple connections at the same time, all free.

![QueryEditor interface](/queryeditor.png)

If you're the kind of person who'd rather write SQL than click through menus, give [QueryEditor](https://queryeditor.com) a shot.
